# Interview Preparation Questions

## Core RAG

1. What is RAG and what problem does it solve?
2. Does RAG fully eliminate hallucination? Why or why not?
3. How do you choose a chunking strategy?
4. What is the difference between context precision and context recall?
5. When is hybrid search useful?
6. What is the difference between a bi-encoder and a cross-encoder?
7. How do you decide whether reranking is worth the latency cost?
8. Why is incremental indexing important?

## Agentic AI

1. What is the difference between an agent and a chatbot?
2. Why use LangGraph or another stateful workflow system?
3. What are the risks of tool use?
4. Where should tool permissions be enforced?
5. How do you design memory safely?
6. When is human approval required?

## Production AI

1. What metrics do you monitor in an LLM system?
2. How do you version prompts, models, and indexes?
3. What does AI incident response look like?
4. How do vLLM, TGI, Triton, and hosted APIs compare?
5. What is the difference between prompt caching and KV-cache?
6. How do you build a latency budget?

## Evaluation and Safety

1. How do you build a golden dataset?
2. What do RAGAS and DeepEval help evaluate?
3. How do you design human-in-the-loop evaluation?
4. Are guardrails only prompts?
5. How do you design a privacy-aware RAG system?
6. What fields belong in an audit log?

## System Design

1. A production AI answer was wrong. How do you debug it?
2. Business users say the model is bad. How do you investigate?
3. How do you turn expert feedback into measurable metrics?
4. When would you choose cloud APIs versus self-hosted models?
5. How would you prepare the system for 10x traffic?
