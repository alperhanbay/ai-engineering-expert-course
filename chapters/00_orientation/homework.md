# Homework: Orientation and Expert Roadmap

Graded against `../../syllabus/evaluation_rubric.md`. Outputs under `my_work/`.
This chapter has no code — its deliverables shape the entire course.

## Required

1. **Capstone proposal.** Write `my_work/capstone_proposal.md` for a regulated
   or high-accuracy domain: problem, users (specific, not "people"), corpus and
   its legal usability, success metric (what a good answer looks like and how
   you'd score it), top 3–5 risks, and explicit non-goals. Under ~600 words but
   complete.

2. **Roadmap.** Write `my_work/roadmap.md` mapping each of the 16 chapters to
   the concrete artifact it will leave for *your* capstone, with an evidence
   checkpoint per chapter. No "understand X" entries — name the artifact.

3. **Decision log.** Set up `my_work/decision_log.md` from the template and
   write the first entry: "why this domain?" (context, options, decision,
   tradeoff, evidence).

4. **Templates ready.** Create `my_work/failure_log.md` and
   `my_work/evidence_checklist.md` from the templates so later chapters have
   somewhere to write.

5. **Peer check.** Have someone read your proposal cold and answer: who are the
   users? what does success look like? what's the top risk? Record their answers
   in `my_work/peer_check.md`; if they can't answer, revise the proposal.

## Stretch

6. **Pace commitment.** Pick a track (standard/intensive/weekend) and write a
   dated schedule mapping chapters to weeks in `my_work/schedule.md`.

7. **Corpus dry-run.** Locate and download 3–5 documents from your chosen
   corpus; confirm you can legally use/redistribute them; note format hazards
   (scanned? tables?) for chapter 7.

8. **Risk specificity.** Rewrite any generic risk ("safety") into a concrete,
   domain-specific one ("a hallucinated filing deadline causes a missed claim").

## Acceptance

- A peer can state your users, success metric, and top risk after one read.
- The roadmap names a concrete artifact per chapter, not a vague goal.
- The decision log has at least one real entry.
- The chosen corpus is confirmed legally usable.
